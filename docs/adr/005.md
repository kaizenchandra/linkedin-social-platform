# 005: Reliable events

Accepted 2026-09-27.

Business mutation and outbox insert share one transaction. Relay holds Oracle row locks with SKIP LOCKED while awaiting broker acknowledgement, then marks delivered. Crash after acknowledgement may duplicate. Consumer inserts dedup record and notification in one Oracle transaction. Finite Kafka retries then DLT; replay preserves eventId. Notifications are historical actions, no live lookup required.
